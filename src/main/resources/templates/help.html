Getting Started

Visit the Registration page, select Donor as your role, fill in your organization details, and submit. You'll receive a verification notice and can log in immediately.

Donors upload food surplus and track its journey. NGOs browse available donations nearby, accept missions, and collect food for redistribution. Admins manage the entire platform ecosystem.

After login, a secure token is stored in your browser's local storage. This token is automatically sent with every API request. It expires after 24 hours — simply log in again to refresh it.
Donations & Food Rescue

Navigate to Upload Food from the sidebar. Fill in the title, category, quantity, expiry time, and pickup address. Optionally use the AI Vision Assistant to auto-fill by uploading a photo of the food.

Nearby NGOs are notified in real-time via WebSocket. They can browse, view details, and accept your donation. You'll receive an in-app notification and can track status in Donation History.

Upload a clear photo of the food. Our AI analyzes the image, detects food type, estimates quantity in kg, assesses freshness, and checks safety status — then auto-fills your donation form with these values.
